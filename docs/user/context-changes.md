# Context changes

Open **Context changes** beside **Current context** in a conversation, or from enterprise settings. On mobile, open **Settings → Environments → [your environment] → Context changes**. The inbox shows changes you authored, changes awaiting your review, and resolved history. Open a proposal to read its reason, reviewers, and attached files.

To propose files, choose **Ingest files**, select a personal recipient, an accessible area, or company, enter a reason, and pick files from your device. Files are uploaded to the connected enterprise hub, including over remote connections. This stages a proposal; it does **not** update current knowledge or agent context.

Only the personal recipient can approve or reject personal proposals. Area leads for that area (and admins) review area proposals; admins review company proposals. Authors can track their submissions even when they cannot decide them. An approved file upload displays **Approved, awaiting processing**: its contents remain out of current context until a future ingestion process handles them. Rejection also leaves current context unchanged.
